## Chapter Goal

After this chapter, the reader can walk through end-to-end interview
scenarios that combine system design, operations, and team leadership,
producing answers that show Tech Lead thinking under realistic
constraints.

## Why This Matters for a Tech Lead

Tech Lead interviews increasingly mix system design, incident
response, and team scenarios in a single conversation. This chapter
trains the connective tissue.

## Theoretical Foundation

> TODO: Cover the structure to use for any scenario:
>
> 1. Clarify the prompt and constraints.
> 2. State assumptions.
> 3. Propose a high-level plan.
> 4. Identify risks and trade-offs.
> 5. Address follow-ups by adjusting the plan, not abandoning it.
> 6. Wrap with what you would do next given more time.
