## Chapter Goal

After this chapter, the reader can communicate technical decisions in
writing and in conversation, manage stakeholders, give and receive
feedback, and disagree productively.

## Why This Matters for a Tech Lead

Soft skills are not "soft" at this level. They are the multiplier that
turns technical decisions into shipped systems. Most Tech Lead
interviews probe these skills as much as technical depth.

## Common Mistakes

> TODO:
>
> 1. Long meetings instead of clear written proposals.
> 2. Feedback that is either vague or personal.
> 3. Disagreeing in chat instead of in the design doc.
> 4. Skipping post-mortems because "we know what happened".
> 5. Treating cross-functional partners as inconveniences.

## Tech Lead Checklist

- [ ] Major decisions have an ADR or RFC.
- [ ] Incidents end with a blameless post-mortem.
- [ ] Feedback is given timely and specifically.
- [ ] Cross-team dependencies have a single owner.
- [ ] Personal calendar protects deep work time.
