## Chapter Goal

After this chapter, the reader can articulate the Tech Lead role,
plan and break down projects, identify and mitigate risks, run
estimates that survive reality, and operate as a technical owner of
a team's outcomes.

## Why This Matters for a Tech Lead

This is the meta-chapter. The reader is preparing to **be** a Tech
Lead. The chapter must ground the role in concrete decisions,
artifacts, and behaviors, not buzzwords.
