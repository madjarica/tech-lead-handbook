## Chapter Goal

After this chapter, the reader has a curated bank of Tech Lead
interview questions across all major topic areas, with strong-vs-weak
answers and explanations of what each question is really testing.

## Why This Matters for a Tech Lead

This chapter is the **drill set**. It is meant to be opened the week
before an interview to rehearse out loud. It complements, not
replaces, the topic chapters.

## Mental Model

> TODO: Three layers of questioning:
>
> 1. Knowledge ("define X").
> 2. Trade-offs ("when not X?").
> 3. Leadership ("how would you decide on X for a team?").
>
> Most Tech Lead interviews test all three.

## Common Mistakes

> TODO: Generic interview anti-patterns:
>
> 1. Jumping to a solution without clarifying.
> 2. Listing technologies instead of trade-offs.
> 3. Avoiding the failure-mode discussion.
> 4. Over-claiming familiarity with version-specific features.
> 5. Not asking interviewer's constraints.
